2. Draw Handling in Xiangqi
This is crucial for Xiangqi! Unlike Western chess where a draw is often equal, in Xiangqi:

Red has first-move advantage (approximately 52-55% win rate in professional games)

Therefore:

Red draws with Black: Red loses ~3-5 ELO, Black gains ~3-5 ELO
Black draws with Red: Considered a good result for Black
This is because drawing as Black against the first-move advantage is actually an achievement.

Expected Score (Black) = 1 / (1 + 10^((Red_ELO - Black_ELO + 25) / 400))

Note: +25 points added to Black's calculation to account for Red's first-move advantage

4. Rating Change Formula
New Rating = Old Rating + K × (Actual Score - Expected Score)

Where Actual Score:

- Draw as Black = 0.55 (slightly rewarded)
- Loss = 0.0
